Format Drift Comparison

Items where format drift disappeared but the answer still stayed wrong

This page keeps only the cases where the early `qwen base sft` output shows obvious format drift, while the later `dapo` or `grpo` output is cleanly terminated but still incorrect. The point is to separate two stages of failure: first the model loses output boundaries, then the boundary becomes stable, but the reasoning is still wrong.

4 comparison items
SFT visible trailing junk or repeated continuation after `Final Answer:`
DAPO / GRPO cleaner endings, but at least one later checkpoint is still wrong

Selected items

The four items below cover two arithmetic word problems and two theorem-style prompts. They are representative because the early stage clearly has a formatting problem, but the later stage has already moved on to content errors.

Reading the comparison

These cases show a clear stage transition. The early model often has two problems at the same time: it gives a wrong answer and also continues generating junk after `Final Answer:`. The later models usually solve the output-boundary issue. However, solving that boundary issue does not automatically fix semantic interpretation, combinatorics setup, or linear algebra reasoning. In this sense, the later checkpoints fail more cleanly, but they still fail.
